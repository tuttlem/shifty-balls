# Desktop Half-Pipe Race Contract

Select `4` to enter the normal four-racer SHIFT race. Countdown, fair release, place, timer, ordered route, result, and rematch remain unchanged.

The new section visibly contains a flat low channel, symmetric higher banks, outer containment, and one forward exit. Low and higher lines may differ only through ordinary gravity, momentum, contact, and existing internal-mass control. No direct movement, auto-centering, teleport, collision attack, scripted line, catch-up, or AI strategy is permitted.

Traffic may change line/order but cannot bypass ordered progression. The camera must leave the player able to identify an exit or recovery direction. `R` must restore the existing fair start and clear all mutable race state.

Excluded: a reusable track system, curved mesh pipeline, alternate routes, laps, new modes, multiplayer, AI avoidance/overtaking, new control models, automatic recovery, bowl/funnel/jump/drop, and persistent records.
